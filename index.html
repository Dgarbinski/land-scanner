What the analysis considers

Landform clues associated with water concentration.

Springs and seeps are controlled by geology and groundwater, but terrain can reveal places where subsurface water is more likely to converge or reach the surface.

  • Local slope and slope breaks
  • Concavity and hollow-like terrain
  • Flow-concentration tendencies
  • Lower-slope and toe-slope position

Questions

What Land Scanner can—and cannot—tell you.

Does Land Scanner detect underground water directly?

No. It analyzes terrain characteristics that can help identify areas worth checking for springs or seeps.

Will the highest score always have water?

No. A high score means the terrain matches more of the selected landform clues. Geology and field conditions still determine whether water is present.

Can I save my analysis?

Yes. Land Scanner is designed so you can retain analyses and candidate locations for later field walking and navigation.
