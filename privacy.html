Privacy Policy & Support

Privacy

Land Scanner does not collect, store, or share personal data.

Any device permissions used by the app are requested only when needed for app functionality. Land Scanner is designed as a terrain-screening utility and does not sell personal information.

Support

For questions, feedback, or technical issues with Land Scanner, contact dgarbinski@gmail.com.

Terrain-screening disclaimer

Land Scanner identifies candidate areas using terrain clues. It does not directly detect groundwater and does not guarantee that a spring or seep exists at any candidate location. On-site validation is recommended before excavation, construction, water-supply decisions, or other consequential actions.
